# C-Star AI Hub | Agent Privacy Lab

**Helping people stay in control as AI agents act.**

C-Star AI Hub is an independent, early-stage research and builder initiative working with Chinese-speaking developers. We study how people can see what data an agent shares, approve actions within a defined scope, and verify what happened. We publish experiments and teaching materials so developers can test those boundaries for themselves.

## What exists today

[Privacy Boundary Lab](../initiatives/ai-ethereum-builder-commons/README.md) uses a simulated agent scenario and two transactions on a local Ethereum development chain. Maintainers verified that the baseline calldata exposed a synthetic note and that the revised payload omitted it. The code, tests, and Chinese walkthrough are public. Independent reproduction is pending.

Other work includes [AI Incident Relay](https://github.com/0xClareYang/ai-incident-relay), a standalone evidence-tracing demo for fact-checking AI responses, and the [August 2026 AI × Midnight Builder Meetup in Hangzhou](../events/2026-08-22-ai-midnight-builder-meetup-hangzhou.md).

## Next milestone

Open agent tooling and selective disclosure are possible research settings. [Validation plan](../initiatives/ai-ethereum-builder-commons/PILOT.md)
